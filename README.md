Perennial is a calm, private home for your notes, grids and plans. Write something down once and it's there on your phone, your computer and the web, quietly in sync.

## What the app does

- **Rich notes:** headings, checklists, highlights, tables, images and voice typing
- **Grids:** simple spreadsheets with formulas for budgets, trackers and lists
- **Calendar:** turn notes into events, set reminders and bring in Google Calendar
- **Live sharing:** invite people as editors or viewers and write together in real time
- **Hidden notes:** hold the logo to open a private space, and hold again to return
- **Optional AI helpers:** grammar fixes, tidy lists, summaries and text-to-event, off until you turn them on
- **Everywhere:** Android, Windows, Mac and the web, with no ads and no tracking

Perennial is in **closed testing on Google Play**. To join, follow the steps on the [website](https://neonamagashi.github.io/Perennial/#join).

## About this repo

This repo is Perennial's landing page, served with GitHub Pages. It's a single `index.html` with no build step:

- **Home:** what Perennial is, how to join the test, and the desktop download
- **Privacy** and **Terms:** in-page tabs, linkable as `#privacy` and `#terms`
- **Assets:** the icons, phone screenshots and desktop demo video are in `assets/`

The button links (Google group, Play testing, web app and desktop download) are set in the `LINKS` block at the bottom of `index.html`.
